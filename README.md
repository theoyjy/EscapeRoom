# 🧩 Escape Room Puzzle Simulator

An immersive Unreal Engine 5 game where players must escape their own dream by solving a series of interconnected puzzles through exploration, logic, and interaction with the environment.

## 🎮 Overview

**Escape Room Puzzle Simulator** is a first-person puzzle experience built in **Unreal Engine 5**, where the player navigates two surreal dream environments. The objective is to wake up by uncovering hidden clues, combining items, and solving symbolic puzzles, all under time constraints and narrative tension.

> "You are trapped in your own dream... and the only way out is to solve the puzzle rooms your mind created."

## 🧠 Game Features

- **Two Distinct Puzzle Levels**:
  - *Level 1*: A guided tutorial where players learn to collect and use items.
  - *Level 2*: A complex room with multi-step puzzles including hidden messages, item combinations, misleading clues, and password-based locks.

- **Core Mechanics**:
  - Inventory system with item pickup and combination
  - Environmental interactions (doors, boxes, notes, clues)
  - NPC guidance
  - Dynamic lighting and world-shattering visual effects
  - Footstep and interaction sounds
  - Start cinematic (in standalone mode)

- **Controls**:
  - Movement: `WASD`
  - Interact: `E`
  - Toggle: `F`

## 🛠 Development Details

- **Engine**: Unreal Engine 5
- **Architecture**: Blueprint-based logic with MVC pattern in bag system
- **Scene Design**:
  - Level 1: Puzzle introduction with NPC guide and door mechanism
  - Level 2: Deceptive props, puzzle paper burning, password deduction, final escape

- **Visual & Audio**:
  - Custom depth stencil highlighting
  - World-shattering ending VFX
  - Footstep and interaction sounds
  - Opening cinematic movie
